Edge cases
Thread moving during drafting: the reply goes out anyway; the new message gets its answer right after, once the first is delivered. At most two answers per burst. No refused.
Silence: inbox done, outcome silent, event silent. Nothing is replayed.
Crash or failure of respond(): attempts counted before the run; on the 2nd pickup, failed and alert. No crash loop.
Crash or timeout while sending: never resent; owner alert. Stuck model call: abandoned after 10 min, retried later.
Re-run after a crash: acted events are re-read, Steve does not redo what it already did.
system (reminder, GitHub): handled alone, never owner; cap of 20 bot/system per thread within 1 h, excluding task reports.
Transient failure (529, quota, network): timer retry with backoff, no attempt counted; the Slack reconnection restarts the outbound.
The tablesinbox and outbox hold state (status UPDATE); events keeps the history and replaces the current log (activity). Rule: transition(row, status, event) does the UPDATE and the INSERT in the same transaction.

reminders stays a mutable table {kind reminder | job, job, due_at, note, address, repeat_minutes, created_by}: cancel = DELETE, re-arm = UPDATE. A single timer on the next due time (capped at 24 days, re-armed on every change). When due, a single transaction creates the system inbox row (source_id reminder:id:due_at) and re-arms or deletes the reminder. A job (memory review, scenarios) calls a named function, outside inbox, invisible to tools. held is for e-mails Steve is unsure about and for drafts on the owner's behalf; polls go through send; a permission a code task lacks is refused (no card to the owner). The catch-up cursor lives in the cursors table (retries on the same timer).
